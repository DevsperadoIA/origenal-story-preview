Read the market

Where can your product create value in Europe?

Market & go-to-market intelligence

Why it matters

Your product is successful in your home market, and you're ready to grow internationally. Remember your product is only the starting point.

To grow successfully across borders, you need to understand the world around you: where it can create value, which markets and channels to explore, who to connect with, and what expertise you need to move forward.

We help you explore the opportunity step by step: from understanding the market and identifying gaps to checking requirements, finding the right channel and adapting your story for your new market.

When deeper specialist expertise is required, we bring together trusted European partners in nutrition, science, regulation, claims and certifications, ensuring the right expertise is connected to your expansion project.

What we can support with

Market and category intelligence

Competitive and opportunity mapping

Consumer and market trends

Regulatory and certification landscape

Nutrition, scientific and claims expertise through specialist partners

Market-entry assessment

Go-to-market recommendations

Step by step

01Explore the marketWhat is happening, and where should you look?

We start by looking at the markets and categories relevant to your product or ingredient.

We look at what is changing, what people value, how the category is evolving and where demand is developing.

We explore
Market dynamicsCategory trendsMarket size & maturityConsumer expectationsRelevant segmentsEmerging trends

What to expect. Market and category intelligence, relevant market comparison and an initial view of where your product could fit.

Best for. Companies considering European expansion and needing to understand where to start.

02Identify the gaps & opportunitiesWhere could your product create value?

Understanding a market is only the beginning. We look for the spaces where your offer can genuinely bring something different or solve an existing need.

We compare markets, categories and existing offers to identify where your product or ingredient could have the strongest potential.

We assess
Market attractivenessCategory fitConsumer demandMarket gapsCompetitive dynamicsProduct benchmarksPositioningPricing contextDifferentiation opportunities

What to expect. Opportunity mapping, competitive benchmarking, market prioritisation and recommendations on where your offer could create the strongest value.

Best for. Companies that need to decide which markets, categories or opportunities deserve their attention and investment.

03Understand your new universeWho and what will shape your opportunity?

Every market has its own ecosystem. Understanding it means looking beyond the product to the people, culture, behaviours, category codes and commercial environment around it.

We help you understand how your product may be perceived, used and valued, and who or what can influence its success.

We explore
Consumer behavioursConsumption occasionsCultural signalsCategory codesLocal expectationsCompetitorsBuyersCommercial playersMarket ecosystem

What to expect. Consumer and cultural insights, competitive context, market ecosystem mapping and a clearer picture of the people and dynamics surrounding your opportunity.

Best for. Companies entering a market where they need to understand the new universe their product is entering, not simply the market statistics.

04Check claims and verificationsWhat do you need to validate before you move forward?

A product that works in one market may face different expectations or requirements in another.

This can include reviewing the regulatory and certification landscape and identifying areas that need technical or scientific validation before market entry.

Trusted European specialists in
NutritionScienceFormulationRegulationClaimsCertifications

What to expect. Regulatory and certification landscape, identification of relevant claims or technical questions, and access to specialist European expertise where required.

Best for. Companies that need to make sure their product, claims and technical information are ready for the target market.

05Identify your channel & adapt your storyHow will your product reach the market, and how should you present it?

The right opportunity also depends on how you reach the market and how you make your offer relevant.

At the same time, we look at how your story, information and positioning may need to adapt to the new context: from brand positioning and storytelling to nutritional information, claims, cosmetics insights or other category-specific expectations, depending on your offer.

Commercial routes
RetailFoodserviceDistributorsImportersManufacturersDirect B2BStrategic partnerships

What to expect. Route-to-market recommendations, channel priorities, initial commercial direction, and recommendations for adapting your brand story, product information and market-facing communication.

Best for. Companies that know they want to enter a market and need to understand how to reach it and how to present themselves once they get there.
