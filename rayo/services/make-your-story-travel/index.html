Services · 02

Make your story travel

Your story may be clear at home. Will it stay the same when you export it?

Brand positioning & storytelling

Why it matters

Entering a new country isn't just about translating your website or tweaking a few messages.

It is about understanding what makes your brand distinctive, what matters to your new audience, and how to express your value in a way that feels relevant locally.

Origenal Story helps you adapt your brand story, value proposition and commercial narrative to new markets: keeping your identity while making your story resonate with local buyers, partners and audiences.

Step by step

01Tap into your Origenal StoryBrand & value proposition

Before adapting your story to another market, you need to be clear about what makes you relevant and distinctive.

We identify your brand's strongest elements and translate them into a clear international value proposition.

We look at
Brand essenceValue propositionDifferentiatorsProduct / ingredient benefitsProof pointsBrand purpose

Best for. Companies preparing to introduce an existing brand or product into a new market.

02Chameleonise your storyBrand storytelling & international market adaptation

Keep your identity. Your story doesn't need to become something else to work in a new market. It needs to become relevant.

We adapt your narrative to local culture, language, category codes and commercial realities, without losing the distinctive identity that makes your brand yours.

We look at
Cultural relevanceLocal language & toneCategory codesBuyer expectationsMarket-specific messagingCommunication nuances

Best for. Brands entering France, Spain or other European markets that need their story to resonate locally.

03Define your own commercial storyB2B storytelling & sales narrative

Your story needs to work beyond your website.

We turn your value proposition into a commercial narrative that distributors, buyers, partners and industry professionals can understand and use.

We can develop
B2B value propositionElevator pitchCompany storyProduct / ingredient narrativeSales argumentsPresentation messagingTrade-fair messaging

Best for. Companies preparing to approach European buyers, distributors, partners or industry events.

04Your story is aliveContent, visibility & market activation

A story only creates value when it enters the world.

We help translate your new narrative into the touchpoints where your market actually encounters your brand, from trade fairs and industry events to B2B2C communications, PR and international networking.

We can support
Trade-fair storytellingB2B2C contentPR narrativesLinkedIn communicationCorporate presentationsEvent messagingPartner communicationsConference / speaking narratives

Best for. Brands ready to turn international positioning into visibility, conversations and commercial opportunities.
