Why it matters

Connecting with the right distributor, importer, foodservice operator, retailer, or strategic partner can make the difference between having a product in a market and actually building a business there.

Origenal Story helps you identify the most relevant routes to market, commercial players and target accounts, and turn market intelligence into a practical business-development roadmap.

For companies entering or growing in Europe, attending or exhibiting at a trade fair can be one of the fastest ways to meet the right buyers, test your proposition and create commercial traction, if the key decision-makers are identified and contacted before, during and after the event.
