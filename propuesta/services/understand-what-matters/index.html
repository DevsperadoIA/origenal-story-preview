FAQ

Questions we often hear.

What is the difference between market intelligence and consumer insight?

Market intelligence tells you where the opportunity is. Consumer insight tells you why people would choose you: their needs, behaviours, motivations, expectations and consumption occasions. You need both to enter a new market with a proposition that resonates.

Why can't I use the same proposition that works in my home market?

Because consumer behaviour does not exist in isolation. Culture, lifestyle and category codes shape what feels relevant, desirable and credible in each country. What is an everyday product at home can be a discovery, a premium treat or a functional product somewhere else.

What do I get at the end of this work?

Consumer and market understanding translated into something you can use: consumer propositions, concept ideas, usage occasions, messages and experiences that can be developed and tested in your target market.

Can you test my product with real consumers before a launch?

Yes. We help bring your proposition to life through tastings, events, partnerships, pop-ups and other consumer and professional touchpoints, gathering feedback and learning from real interactions.

Is this only for consumer brands, or also for ingredient and B2B companies?

For both. If you sell an ingredient or a B2B product, understanding the final consumer helps you make a stronger case to manufacturers, distributors and buyers. That is what we mean by B2B2C.
