04

Understand what matters

Activate what resonates.

Entering a new market is not only about understanding the category or finding the right commercial partners.

It is about understanding the people behind the market: what they value, how they behave, what influences their choices and how those expectations are evolving.

Origenal Story helps you turn consumer and market insight into relevant opportunities, propositions and activations: connecting what people want with how your brand can engage with them locally.

We can help you

Understand your consumers
Identify needs, behaviours, motivations, expectations and consumption occasions.
Decode local market dynamics
Understand how culture, lifestyle and category codes influence consumer choices.
Identify opportunities
Translate consumer and market signals into relevant product, proposition and activation opportunities.
Test & learn
Gather market feedback and refine your proposition before scaling.
Activate the market
Bring your proposition to life through experiences, events, partnerships, content and B2B2C initiatives.
