01

Read the market

Where can your product create value in Europe?

Your product is successful in your home market, and you're ready to grow internationally. Remember your product is only the starting point.

To grow successfully across borders, you need to understand the world around you: where it can create value, which markets and channels to explore, who to connect with, and what expertise you need to move forward.

We help you explore the opportunity step by step: from understanding the market and identifying gaps to checking requirements, finding the right channel and adapting your story for your new market.

When deeper specialist expertise is required, we bring together trusted European partners in nutrition, science, regulation, claims and certifications, ensuring the right expertise is connected to your expansion project.

What we can support with

  • Market and category intelligence
  • Competitive and opportunity mapping
  • Consumer and market trends
  • Regulatory and certification landscape
  • Nutrition, scientific and claims expertise through specialist partners
  • Market-entry assessment
  • Go-to-market recommendations

How we work on it.

We start by looking at the markets and categories relevant to your product or ingredient.

03

Understand your new universe

Who and what will shape your opportunity?

Every market has its own ecosystem. Understanding it means looking beyond the product to the people, culture, behaviours, category codes and commercial environment around it.

We help you understand how your product may be perceived, used and valued, and who or what can influence its success.

We explore
Consumer behavioursConsumption occasionsCultural signalsCategory codesLocal expectationsCompetitorsBuyersCommercial playersMarket ecosystem
What to expect
Consumer and cultural insights, competitive context, market ecosystem mapping and a clearer picture of the people and dynamics surrounding your opportunity.
Best for
Companies entering a market where they need to understand the new universe their product is entering, not simply the market statistics.
04

Check claims and verifications

What do you need to validate before you move forward?

A product that works in one market may face different expectations or requirements in another.

This can include reviewing the regulatory and certification landscape and identifying areas that need technical or scientific validation before market entry.

At the same time, we look at how your story, information and positioning may need to adapt to the new context: from brand positioning and storytelling to nutritional information, claims, cosmetics insights or other category-specific expectations, depending on your offer.

FAQ

Questions we often *hear*.

How do I know if my product is ready for the European market?

That is the first question this service answers. A product that works in one market may face different expectations or requirements in another. We compare markets, categories and existing offers to see where your product or ingredient has the strongest potential, and what you need to validate before moving forward.

What does a European market opportunity study include?

We look at market dynamics, category trends, market size and maturity, consumer expectations, the competitive landscape and the regulatory and certification landscape. We close with a market-entry assessment and go-to-market recommendations you can act on.

Do you cover regulation, claims and certifications?

We review the regulatory and certification landscape and identify what needs technical or scientific validation before market entry. When deeper specialist expertise is required, we bring in trusted European partners in nutrition, science, regulation, claims and certifications.

Which products and sectors do you work with?

Food and ingredients, gourmet and food service, well-being and cosmetics, and lifestyle and premium brands. We work with producers and brands from the Global South, especially Brazil and Latin America, that want to grow in Europe, and with European companies opening new markets.

Which European markets do you cover?

We are based in Barcelona and work across Europe. We have worked with clients in Spain, France, Belgium, the Netherlands, Germany, Italy, Portugal, Switzerland, Denmark and the United Kingdom.

How do we start?

With a short conversation. Answer the three questions of our quiz and book a 1:1 call with Claudia Vasquez, founder of Origenal Story, to agree on the right starting point for your project.
