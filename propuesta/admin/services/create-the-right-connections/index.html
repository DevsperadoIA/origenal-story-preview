Identify the optimal route to market.

FAQ

Questions we often *hear*.

How do I find the right distributor or importer in Europe?

Before looking for a partner, we look at how your product can reach the market: retail, foodservice, distributors, importers, manufacturers or direct B2B. Then we map the companies that fit your offer, review their profile and portfolio fit, and prioritise the ones worth your time.

Do you only deliver a list of contacts?

No. A list is only the beginning. We help you qualify and prioritise target accounts, identify the people behind the buying and partnership decisions, and structure the conversations so that relevant contacts become qualified next steps.

Is it worth exhibiting at a trade fair such as SIAL Paris, Anuga or Biofach?

Attending or exhibiting at a trade fair can be one of the fastest ways to meet the right buyers, test your proposition and create commercial traction, as long as the key decision-makers are identified and contacted before, during and after the event. We help you choose where to show up, prepare your proposition and follow up afterwards.

Which trade fairs and events do you work with?

We follow the main food, ingredient, organic and natural cosmetics events in Europe, such as SIAL Paris, Anuga, Alimentaria, Biofach, Natexpo, Fruit Logistica and in-cosmetics Global, as well as sector conferences and summits.

We already work with a distributor in Europe. Can you still help?

Yes. If you are already active in Europe and want to scale, we review your current route to market, identify additional channels and target accounts, and help you prioritise where to grow next.
