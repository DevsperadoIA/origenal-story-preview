Interior Luxury Design Brand — European Market Orientation

European market orientation for a luxury interior design brand. Barcelona recommended as strategic base, aligned with the brand's aesthetic, audience and commercial-cultural resonance.

ServicePartner & Lead Activation
MarketEU
SectorPremium craft, Design
Year2024

The challenge

The brand needed guidance on where to position itself in Europe and which city could offer the strongest strategic fit for visibility, relevance and connection with the right audience for its sculptural design universe.

The solution

Origenal Story assessed the market context and recommended Barcelona as a strategic location, based on its fit with the brand's aesthetic, audience and potential for commercial and cultural resonance. The project helped shape a more focused European market-entry direction.

In their words

Origenal Story helped us see the European opportunity more clearly and identify Barcelona as a strategic base aligned with our brand universe and ambitions.
