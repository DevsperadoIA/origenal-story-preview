Natural & Organic Madrid — Event Activation for Brazilian & French Markets

Event activation as sales agent for the Brazilian and French markets around the Madrid edition of Natural & Organic Products. Targeted outreach and market-facing promotion across both markets.

ServiceEvent & Press Activation
MarketBrazil & France → Spain
SectorNatural & organic, Food, Cosmetics
Year2024

The challenge

The event needed stronger commercial activation to increase its relevance and visibility among target audiences in the Brazilian and French markets, while also supporting sales outreach and international engagement around the Madrid edition.

The solution

As sales agent for the Brazilian and French markets, Origenal Story supported the international activation of the event through targeted outreach, market-facing promotion and connection-building with relevant professional audiences. The work helped strengthen the event's visibility and commercial reach across both markets.
