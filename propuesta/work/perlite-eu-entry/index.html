Perlite Project — Market Entry Strategy & Sector Targeting

Market entry strategy and sector targeting for the European Perlite opportunity. Clear view of relevance, sector fit and entry priorities — moving the client from assumptions to a strategic view.

ServicePartner & Lead Activation
MarketEU
SectorSustainable agriculture, Sector input materials
Year2024

The challenge

The project required a clearer view of how to approach the European market, identify the most relevant opportunities and understand which sector players could support commercial entry.

The solution

Origenal Story carried out market assessment work linked to the Perlite opportunity, helping define market relevance, sector fit and practical entry priorities. This included identifying relevant commercial pathways and key sector contacts to support a more informed market-entry approach.

In their words

Origenal Story helped us move from assumptions to a much clearer view of where the opportunity was and how to approach it more strategically.

Perlite project lead

What we delivered

  1. Market relevance and sector fit assessment
  2. Practical entry priorities
  3. Key sector contacts mapped
