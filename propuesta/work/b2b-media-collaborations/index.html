International B2B Media Collaborations — Advertising Activation Across Trade Publications

Advertising and visibility activation through specialised B2B trade publications — Eurofresh Distribution (Europe), Portal Frutícola (Chile), European Dairy Magazine (Germany), Organic Wellness (Canada). Credible professional reach for sector campaigns.

ServiceEvent & Press Activation
MarketEurope, Chile, Germany, Canada
SectorB2B Media, Trade publications
Year2024

The challenge

Brands and sector initiatives needed stronger professional visibility across international markets, with activation through trusted B2B trade media able to reach targeted audiences in food, fresh produce, dairy and related sectors.

The solution

Origenal Story developed advertising and visibility activation through collaborations with specialised B2B trade publications such as Eurofresh Distribution in Europe, Portal Frutícola in Chile, European Dairy Magazine in Germany and Organic Wellness in Canada. This helped connect campaigns and commercial messages with relevant international sector audiences through credible professional channels.

In their words

Origenal Story brought valuable international media connections with key brands and partners and helped us activate visibility through the right professional publications.
