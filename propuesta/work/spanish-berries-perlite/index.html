Spanish Berry Producers / Perlite

Spanish Berries / Perlite — Market Assessment

Grounded market assessment for a Perlite opportunity linked to Spanish berry producers. Research-based work with local contacts and a local call centre agency turned assumptions into informed decisions.

ServiceMarket Intelligence
MarketSpain
SectorFresh produce, Sustainable agriculture
Year2024

The challenge

The project required a grounded understanding of market interest, sector fit and commercial relevance for a Perlite-related opportunity connected to Spanish berry producers.

The solution

Origenal Story led a research-based market assessment with local contacts and a local call centre agency, coordinating insight gathering and translating findings into practical conclusions that could support decision-making and market validation.

What we delivered

  1. Local sector insight gathering
  2. Practical market conclusions
  3. Decision-ready validation
