Lévere

Lévere — Spain Market Access & Distributor Search

Spain market access and distributor search for Lévere (Brazilian nutraceutical). 5 Spanish distributors connected in a single trade fair plus introductions to relevant influencers and importers.

ServicePartner & Lead Activation
MarketBrazil → Spain
SectorNutraceuticals
Year2024

The challenge

The brand needed to accelerate its entry into Spain by gaining access to the right local players, including distributors, importers and market-facing contacts able to support commercial development.

The solution

Origenal Story supported market access by identifying and connecting the CEO with relevant influencers, importers and five Spanish distributors during a trade fair. The work combined market-entry support with direct commercial activation on the ground.

In their words

Origenal Story helped us open the right doors quickly and connect with relevant distributors and contacts in Spain in a very practical way.

Lévere, Brazilian nutraceutical brand

What we delivered

  1. 5 Spanish distributors connected
  2. Influencer and importer introductions
  3. On-ground commercial activation
