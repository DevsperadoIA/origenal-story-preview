Brazilian Açaí Producer & Exporter

EU Market Opportunity Mapping for a Brazilian Açaí Producer

EU market opportunity mapping for a Brazilian açaí producer and exporter. Origenal Story analysed target-market potential, buyer relevance and expansion priorities to support a clearer, more strategic European market entry.

ServiceMarket Intelligence
MarketBrazil → EU
SectorFresh produce, Sustainable agriculture
Year2024

The challenge

The client needed to assess the potential of European markets for Brazilian açaí products and identify where to focus expansion efforts with the strongest commercial relevance.

The solution

Origenal Story carried out EU market opportunity mapping, analysing target-market potential, buyer relevance and expansion priorities to support a clearer and more strategic market-entry approach.

In their words

Origenal Story helped us better understand where the strongest opportunities were in Europe and how to approach them with greater clarity and confidence, as our brand's voice required some adaptation to local markets.

European Export Manager, Brazilian Açaí brand

What we delivered

  1. Target-market potential assessment
  2. Buyer relevance map
  3. Expansion priorities matrix
  4. Strategic market-entry approach
