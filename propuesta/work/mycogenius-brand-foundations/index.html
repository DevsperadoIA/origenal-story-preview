Mycogenius

Mycogenius — Mission, Vision, Values & Brand Foundations

Brand foundations for Mycogenius (premium functional mushrooms). Mission, vision, values, philosophy and positioning aligned with premium quality, natural credibility and consumer trust.

ServiceBrand Positioning
MarketInternational
SectorFunctional ingredients, Nutraceuticals
Year2024

The challenge

The brand needed a clearer strategic foundation to support its growth, express its philosophy with more coherence and position itself credibly for international audiences interested in premium functional mushrooms.

The solution

Origenal Story supported the development and refinement of the brand's mission, vision, values, philosophy and positioning foundations. This work helped shape a more consistent international brand identity aligned with premium quality, natural credibility and consumer trust.

What we delivered

  1. Mission, vision, values
  2. Brand philosophy
  3. Positioning foundations
  4. International brand identity
